Burn widget alternatives

Each design shows one number per quota: the weekly burn multiplier, time left in the week divided by quota left. At 1.0× the quota runs out exactly at reset; above 1.0× it runs out early. Everything else stays behind the click-through to the status page. The buttons switch every design between a calm week, today's numbers, and a week running hot.

Mock-ups with fixed values. The live widget is on the home page.
